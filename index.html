tilt4d

What would you see if you could not just turn left, right, up, and down, but also before and after?

Kept in this browser from one visit to the next, apart from the mode and the pace.

An ordinary 3D renderer shows one flat slice of spacetime: everything as it is right now. Here the world is a single fixed four-dimensional scene, with the whole history of every object laid out at once, and you are a camera inside it. What you see is still a three-dimensional slice of it, but the slice need not be level.

Besides turning left and right and looking up and down, there is one more way to turn: tilting, which swings the view out of space and into time. To your right the slice then rises into the future, to your left it dips into the past (or the other way round, tilted the other way), further the further away: by one second for every 10 m at a tilt of 45°. Your own time slows as you tilt: at a right angle it stands still, and beyond that it runs backwards. You move about as usual, but within the slice you see: a step to the right in a tilted view also takes you on in time, and so does walking forward once you have turned to face that way.

It resembles relativity, but it is not: the rotation is an ordinary Euclidean one and light is instantaneous, so the world stretches along the tilt instead of contracting.
